---

*The Monstrous Beloved* follows six sapphic romances between mortal women and the creatures who fall for them. Each novel has a different central couple, setting, and emotional problem. Across the books, the Registry of Magical Practices connects their encounters to a larger institutional history.

If you want the fullest experience of that history, start with *The Binding Night* and read in publication order. If one particular romance calls to you, the individual books can be read independently. You do not need to postpone the coast or the vampire until you have earned them.

The series is intended for readers eighteen and older. Content notes are available by title, because the kinds of danger and the relationship dynamics differ between books.

## The Six-Book Reading Order

### 1. The Binding Night

Sable summons the wrong thing. Vex has been waiting centuries for the right Carnelian to make that mistake.

Begin here for a demon romance built around a magical bond and the question of what its participants choose afterward. This is also the first place to meet the Registry thread as it enters the lives of the series' couples.

### 2. The Tide Keeps

Petra comes to Cauldmor Research Station hoping to rebuild a career in quiet. Aoibhinn has been waiting on the rocks below it, carrying grief the surviving record cannot adequately explain.

Choose this book for selkie romance, coastal gothic atmosphere, and an investigation shaped by absence. The station and the shore offer different accounts of the same world.

### 3. Night's Own

Nox feeds on dreams. Vesper is a novelist whose work begins turning toward her.

This is the book for readers drawn to creative obsession and the dangerous intimacy of being noticed. Its strix gives the Registry a problem before any investigation can settle comfortably into routine: the creature described as extinct is alive.

### 4. The Vast and Particular

Verity manages the risks of her black-market operation with careful precision. The ancient entity in her basement is harder to fit into those calculations.

Choose this romance for a competent woman encountering something whose scale defeats her accustomed terms. Morwick's house and the work beneath it bring the series into a more confined, intensely intimate space.

### 5. The Turning Season

Elowen returns to Skovdal carrying shame she has mistaken for fact. Tarn stayed, and their shared history includes things neither has managed to say.

This is a homecoming romance with shapeshifting, community pressure, and the consequences of having learned to make yourself smaller. Transformation is treated as a bodily truth, even when the surrounding world has made it difficult to inhabit freely.

### 6. What the Night Keeps

Maren arrives in Selvast as an investigative journalist. Lucrezia is a vampire who has kept records the Registry would prefer to leave buried.

Choose it for vampire romance, investigative work, and intimacy built through disclosure. It also closes the series' wider thread, making publication order especially rewarding if you want to follow that thread as it develops.

## Individual Novels or Omnibus Editions?

The collected editions contain the same six novels, divided into two volumes. They provide another way to read the series, rather than an additional step in the reading order.

**Volume I** collects *The Binding Night*, *The Tide Keeps*, and *Night's Own*: Books One through Three.

**Volume II** collects *The Vast and Particular*, *The Turning Season*, and *What the Night Keeps*: Books Four through Six.

For a first read, the collected route is Volume I followed by Volume II. If you already own individual novels, you do not need an omnibus to continue the story. Pick up whichever book comes next for you.

## Find the Right Kind of Darkness

A shared series label does not make every romance identical. Bargains, grief, hunger, institutional power, shame, and disclosure put different pressures on the people involved.

The content-notes page gives book-specific information, including distinctions in consent and power dynamics. It is there to help you choose an experience that suits you. You can read those notes before deciding where to begin without needing to explore the lore archive first.

If you are still undecided, start with *The Binding Night*. It opens the series' first door, and the remaining books will give you plenty of reasons to keep going.
